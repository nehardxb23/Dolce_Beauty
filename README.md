### 📖 About

Dolce Beauty is a modern beauty e-commerce platform built around one bold idea:

> **What if ordering makeup was as fast, exciting, and satisfying as ordering a hot pizza?**

Inspired by Gen Z's love for instant experiences, Dolce Beauty combines **10-minute beauty delivery**, premium cosmetics, and **pizza-themed packaging** to create an unforgettable shopping experience.

Every order is designed to feel like opening your favorite pizza box—except inside is your favorite beauty collection.
